Currency Lens is a currency converter for web pages: select a price such as $19.99, €49 or 1,200 JPY, click the small button next to it, and see the amount in your own currencies without leaving the page.

It is handy when shopping on overseas sites, planning a trip, or reading prices in foreign news and price lists. The page itself is never rewritten; the result appears in a small card next to your selection.

How it works
1. Open Currency Lens from the toolbar and choose up to 5 currencies to convert into.
2. Select text that contains a price on any web page.
3. Click the Currency Lens button that appears next to your selection.
4. A card shows the price converted into each of your currencies.

Features
- Price detection: finds amounts written with currency symbols ($, €, ¥), ISO codes (USD, JPY) or currency names ("dollars", "円"), including scales such as "5k", "$1.5 million" and "5万円".
- Up to 5 target currencies: add, remove and reorder them from the toolbar popup.
- Built-in price converter: type any amount and currency in the popup to convert it by hand.
- Re-convert on the spot: change the amount or the source currency in the card.
- Ambiguous symbols: "$" and similar symbols are resolved from your settings, the page language and the browser language, and you can choose how each symbol is read.
- Exchange rates: rates from Open Exchange Rates are refreshed every hour. If a refresh fails, the last rates are kept, and a warning appears once they are more than 24 hours old.
- English and Japanese interface. No account needed.

Privacy
Selected text is analyzed inside the extension and never sent anywhere. The only network request fetches exchange rates and contains no page content.
Privacy policy: https://cl.dryk.net/privacy

Where it works
Regular web pages in desktop Chrome and Firefox 140 or later. It does not work in PDFs, form fields, canvas, browser settings pages or extension stores. If a selection contains several prices, the first one is converted.
