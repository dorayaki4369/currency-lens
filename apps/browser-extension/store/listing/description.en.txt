Currency Lens converts prices right on the page you are reading. Shopping on an overseas site, planning a trip or reading foreign news? Select a price like $19.99, €49 or 1,200 JPY and see it in up to 5 currencies of your choice, without leaving the page or changing it.

How it works

1. Select a price on a web page.
2. Click the Currency Lens button that appears next to your selection.
3. A small card shows the price in your chosen currencies. You can change them anytime from the toolbar.

Features

- Reads prices in many formats: currency symbols ($, €, ¥), codes (USD, JPY) and names ("dollars", "円"), including amounts such as "$1.5 million", "5k円" and "5万円", and prices inside Japanese, Chinese, Korean or Thai sentences.
- Handles shared symbols like "$": when a symbol is shared by several currencies, Currency Lens picks one from your settings, the page language and your browser language. You can change which currency each symbol stands for.
- Edit and re-convert: change the amount or the source currency right in the card.
- Toolbar popup: type any amount and currency to convert it directly, and add, remove or reorder your currencies.
- Rate history charts: in the toolbar popup and the card, see how the rates of your currencies have moved over periods from 1 day to 1 year, or compare the changes of all of them on one chart.
- Latest exchange rates from Open Exchange Rates, with a warning if they are out of date.
- Interface in English, Japanese, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Korean, Spanish, French, German and Brazilian Portuguese, following your browser language.

Privacy

No account needed. Selected text is analyzed locally in your browser and never sent anywhere. The only network request fetches exchange rates and contains no page content.

Privacy policy: https://cl.dryk.net/privacy

Where it works

Regular web pages in desktop Chrome and Microsoft Edge, and in Firefox 140 or later. It does not work on PDFs, form fields, canvas elements, browser settings pages or extension stores.
